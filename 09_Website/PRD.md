# Portfolio PRD

## Users

- Procurement manager يبحث عن تحسين عملية الشراء.
- Supply chain manager يعاني من مخزون أو تخطيط طلب ضعيف.
- SME يحتاج SOP أو dashboard.
- Recruiter أو عميل مستقل يريد التحقق من الخبرة.

## Required sections

1. Header with name, language toggle, and contact CTA.
2. Hero with one-sentence value proposition.
3. Credibility metrics, only verified values.
4. Services grouped by business outcome.
5. Featured case studies.
6. Process: Discover, Diagnose, Design, Deliver.
7. Packages and starting prices.
8. About and experience.
9. FAQ.
10. Contact CTA and footer.

## Non-functional requirements

- Static HTML/CSS/JS is acceptable for MVP.
- Mobile-first and responsive.
- Arabic RTL default with English content ready.
- No external dependency required for core experience.
- Accessible buttons, focus states, labels, and keyboard-close modal.
